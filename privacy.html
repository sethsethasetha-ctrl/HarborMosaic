Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zjiagame (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Harbor Mosaic (the “App”).

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores game progress, level unlocks, stars, collected artwork, and your music preference locally on your device. This information is used to provide the game experience and is not used to identify you.

Advertising, Analytics, and Device Information

The App may use third-party advertising and security services. When enabled and available, these providers may process device identifiers, IP address, device and operating-system information, ad interaction data, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and protect the App. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

On iOS 14 or later, the App requests permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before allowing tracking for advertising purposes. You may decline permission and continue using the App. Ads may still be shown when available, but may be less relevant.

How We Use Information

We use locally stored information to save your game. We and our service providers may use technical information to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the App, and to provide advertising where applicable.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support advertising, app security, or diagnostics, only for the purposes described above or as required by law.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Third-party providers retain information under their own policies. You can reset advertising choices in iOS Settings and manage tracking permission at any time.

Children

The App is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, contact us so we can address the request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@zjiagame.com. Confirm this inbox is monitored before publishing this policy.
