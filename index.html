Relaxing Picture Puzzles

Harbor Mosaic

Restore radiant scenes one piece at a time in a calm, landscape puzzle journey for iPhone and iPad.

Piece Together a World of Art

Study each illustrated scene, then match every fragment to its place. Correct pieces restore the picture, unlock the next level, and add a new work to your collection.

Designed for a Quiet Break

  • 30 visual picture-puzzle levels with a gentle difficulty curve
  • Fantasy gardens, city lights, landscapes, myths, flowers, and birds
  • Local progress, stars, and a collection of restored artwork
  • Relaxing looping music with a simple on/off switch
  • Landscape gameplay for iPhone and iPad running iOS 13 or later

Play Fair

Harbor Mosaic is a visual puzzle game. It contains no number draw, wagering, prize, lottery, or gambling mechanic.
